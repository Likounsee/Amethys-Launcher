## 5. Authorship, credit and planned project rewards

If the contribution is accepted and incorporated, the Recipient intends to
credit the Contributor in CONTRIBUTORS.md and, where reasonably practicable,

Acknowledgement does not reverse the assignment of economic rights validly
made under this agreement.

The project intends to establish a gift-card reward programme after
completion of Amethys Launcher with its Store and integrated wallets. The
intended reward level will reflect accepted contribution and involvement.
Eligibility, criteria, budget, gift-card types, values and distribution
conditions will be published separately before awards are assessed. This
agreement alone does not promise a specific reward, value or date and any
reward does not replace or alter the rights assignment above.

## 8. Review, acceptance and effective date

This agreement does not oblige the Recipient to accept, merge, publish or
exploit the contribution. The assignment becomes effective only when:

1. both parties have validly signed this agreement;
2. the contribution identified in Section 2 is sufficiently specific and
   matches the submitted work; and
3. the Recipient has accepted that contribution in writing.

Until these conditions are met, the contribution must not be merged,
distributed or incorporated into Amethys Launcher.
